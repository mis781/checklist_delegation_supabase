    Botivate script — logic check
    
    
    Aapki Apps Script ke har TAT formula ko 20,000 alag timestamps pe chala ke dekha. Jo gadbad mili wo neeche hai, real example ke saath. Form ka preview aur fixed formula in sab cases ko sahi handle karta hai.
    

    
    
      BugTAT in Hours (inside) — plannedwwh
      
      
        
    • Aaj ke ghante chhod deta hai: 3 PM entry, TAT 5 hr. 3 hr aaj count hone chahiye, baaki 2 hr kal → sahi Thu 12 PM. Script agle din 10 AM se poore 5 hr dobara ginta hai → Thu 3 PM.
      • 
        
    • 6 PM ke baad ka result nahi pakadta: HOUR(...)>$D$1 me 6:30 PM ka HOUR = 18, jo 18 se bada nahi. 3 PM + 3.5 hr = 6:30 PM office band hone ke baad hi planned aa jata hai.
      • 
        
    • Subah jaldi ki entry: 7 AM entry + 3 hr = 10 AM aata hai, jabki counting 10 AM se honi chahiye → sahi 1 PM.
      • 
        
    • Raat ki entry / bada TAT: 9 PM entry + 4 hr → Wed 2 PM, yaani entry se bhi pehle ka time. 16 hr TAT → raat 2 AM.
      • 
        
    • Holidays nahi: is formula ke WORKDAY.INTL me Holidays!A:A missing hai.
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    
      BugTAT in Hours (outside) — plannedwwh2
      
      
        
    • Opening/closing hour formula me hard-code: ofs.toString() number seedha formula me chala jata hai. Baad me C1/D1 badla to purane formule update nahi honge.
      • 
        
    • Raat ki entry galat: 9 PM + 4 hr → Thu 5 PM (sahi Thu 2 PM).
      • 
        
    • Subah ki entry: 7 AM + 3 hr → agle din raat 2:00 AM (sahi usi din 1 PM).
      • 
        
    • Boundary: hour>open strict hai, to 10:xx wala result bhi "bahar" maana jata hai.
      • 
        
    • Minutes: minute(fromDate) liya hai, minute(fromDate+TAT) nahi — TAT 1:30 jaisa ho to minutes galat.
      • 
        
    • 1 din se bada TAT handle nahi hota (16 hr → galat din).
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    
      TheekTAT in days — plannedindays
      
      
        
    • Logic sahi hai: working days + holidays skip, aur timestamp ka time wapas jodta hai.
      • 
        
    • Bas weekly off "0000001" (sirf Sunday) fixed hai. Saturday half/off wale office me badalna padega.

      Dhyan doT-x Formula — plannedlead
      
      
        
    • Formula Date + LeadTime − x hai, yaani calendar days. Sunday/holiday skip nahi hota, isliye planned Sunday pe bhi aa sakta hai.
      • 
        
    • Fix: WORKDAY.INTL(INT(Date+Lead), −x, "0000001", Holidays!A:A).

      Dhyan doSpecific Time — specificTime
      
      
        
    • Chhutti wale din entry: "0 din" par WORKDAY.INTL(date, 0) wahi chhutti wala din de deta hai — planned Sunday ko.
      • 
        
    • Cut-off nahi: 7 PM entry, fixed time 5 PM, 0 din → usi din 5 PM (jo nikal chuka), turant delay dikhega. Form me "cut-off" option isi ke liye hai.
      • 
        
    • Time ko 17/24 jaise format me maangta hai — 5 likh diya to galat result aayega.
      • 
      
    
    
    
    
    
      Dhyan doBaaki functions
      
      
        
    • TAT cell ka format: hours wale formulas TAT ko seedha date me jodte hain, isliye cell me 4:00:00 ya =4/24 hona chahiye, sirf 4 nahi (warna 4 din jud jayenge).
      • 
        
    • timeDelay: kaam pending hai aur planned abhi aaya nahi, tab bhi $A$1 − Planned negative value dikhata hai. IF($A$1>Planned, …, "") lagana chahiye. Conditional formatting ke rules kai baar repeat hain — sirf aakhri wale kaam karte hain.
      • 
        
    • removeTrigger: getUniqueId()==getUniqueId() hamesha true hai, to ye project ka koi bhi pehla trigger delete kar deta hai — dusre triggers bhi ud sakte hain.
      • 
        
    • onChange_new: onChange trigger me getActiveCell() bharosemand nahi (formula/import se change hone par galat cell). Installable onEdit(e) aur e.range better hai. "No" status pe bhi Actual time lag jata hai — check karo ye chahiye ya nahi.
